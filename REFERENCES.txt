## 📈 KEY EQUATIONS REFERENCE
====================
Light Thrust: F = 2(P/c) A η cos²α
- P = 1361 W/m² (1 AU)
- c = 3×10⁸ m/s
- η = 0.99 (SLaSS lattice)
- α = sail pitch angle

Shadow Ablation: ṁ = F/v_e
- v_e = 2000 m/s (thermal propellant)
- Δm_1hr = 0.18 g (100m² sail)

Orbit: Δv = ∫(F/m) dt
- a_light = 90.7 μm/s² (10kg CubeSat)
- Δv_1year = 2.86 km/s continuous
